# Code of Conduct

This project teaches people, often people new to the field, so it should be a place where asking a basic
question is welcome.

## Our standard

- Be kind, patient and specific. Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith; explain rather than dismiss. "Why did you choose that?" beats "that's wrong".
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or unwelcome sexual attention, in any form.
- Respect privacy. Do not share other people's personal information or lab details without permission.
- Keep the security work ethical: discuss and demonstrate attacks only against systems you own or are
  authorised to test. Requests for help attacking third parties will be closed and may be reported.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions, and may restrict participation, when conduct falls
short of this standard. Report concerns privately to the maintainers through a GitHub
[private message to the repository owner](https://github.com/AndyProsser) or by opening a private security
advisory (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)). Reports are handled promptly and confidentially.

This is a short statement written in the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), which you are welcome to read for fuller guidance.

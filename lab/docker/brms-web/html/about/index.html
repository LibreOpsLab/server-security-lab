About Boab Ridge Mining Services

Boab Ridge started in 2011 as a two-person fitting and welding business in Welshpool, Perth. The founders did one thing well: they turned up, fixed the machine and went home with the job recorded properly. Word spread among Pilbara maintenance supervisors.

Our story

  • 2011 Two fitters, one workshop.
  • 2015 The first long-term contract with a Pilbara mine operator.
  • 2019 A remote site office opens in the Pilbara and the company grows to about 40 staff.
  • 2022 A place on the panel of a large mine operator that is part of an international group.
  • 2026 About 60 staff and three client contracts.

Two places, one company

About 45 people work from our Perth head office (management, finance, planning, engineering and IT). About 15 work at our remote site office in the Pilbara, with rostered contractors rotating through on two 12-hour shifts. The site never waits for Perth to wake up.

This company is fictional

Boab Ridge Mining Services does not exist. It is the example business for an open server-security lab: a small company with a head office, a remote site and a handful of servers that students build, attack and then harden. Its people, address, phone number, dates and figures are invented. The phone number is in a range reserved for fiction. Any resemblance to a real business or person is accidental.

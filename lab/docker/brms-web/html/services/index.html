Services

Every job we do is quoted, rostered, carried out, recorded and invoiced. The job record is how we get paid, so we keep it as carefully as we keep the machine.

Fabrication

Steel and equipment repairs made to order: liners, guards, chutes, brackets and replacement parts, built in our Welshpool workshop or on site.

Fleet servicing

Scheduled servicing of heavy equipment on your site, by a rostered crew, with the work logged against each machine.

Shutdown crews

Planned shutdowns run by our planning team: we take over a plant, work to the plan and hand it back on time.

Where we work

Mid-sized and large mine operators in the Pilbara and the Goldfields of Western Australia. Our crews are based in Perth and at our remote site office, and fly in and out on rosters.

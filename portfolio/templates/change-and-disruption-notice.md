# Hazards, change record and disruption notice

Use this before work that could interrupt other people, such as a restart, a firewall change or a migration. It says what will happen, when, who is affected and how to reach you.

## 1. Hazards and controls

| Hazard (physical, workplace, operational) | Likelihood and harm | Control (use the hierarchy of controls) | Owner |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ----- |

## 2. Change record

Description; reason; risk and controls; test plan and results; rollback; schedule.

## 3. Disruption notice (to the people affected)

- **What** is changing, in plain words
- **Who** is affected
- **When** and **how long**
- **What to do instead** during the change
- **Who to contact**
